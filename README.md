# D4 build planner — Barbarian first (Season 15, patch 3.2.1)

Local static app. Not affiliated with Blizzard. The calculator is the v0 damage sheet. The optimizer is milestone 1: a class-agnostic search that currently loads the Barbarian catalog.

## Run

`python3 -m http.server 8080` in this folder (or `npm start`).

- Calculator: http://localhost:8080/
- Optimizer: http://localhost:8080/optimizer.html
- Data entry and the needs-input list: http://localhost:8080/data-entry.html

Tests: `npm test` (Node built-in runner, no dependencies). The script passes `test/*.test.js` so Node 20 and Node 22 both run the files. `npm run needs-input` (the same as `npm run needs-input -- --build user`) splits every null, unverified flag, roll range, and empty collection into three tiers. Engine-blocking is a null or range that makes the current user build's damage or effective-health score wrong or impossible. Required is a coefficient or stat the scorer reads once that block is filled. Nice-to-have is metadata the engine ignores, including descriptions, equipped-item slots, stacking text, glyphs, and paragon. Engine-blocking and required lines are labeled `user-only` (Nikolay's rolls, tooltips, or glyph levels) or `sourceable` (official patch notes or in-game text already in the repo). `allocated: null` is pending allocation. `allocated: false` marks a skill or upgrade inactive, and that node and its fields are left out of every tier.

## Data sourcing rules

Only official Blizzard pages and manual entry from your own game client. No datamines, no client reading, and no copying from Maxroll, Mobalytics, Icy Veins, Wowhead, or d4builds. Never invent numbers: unknown stays `null`, and `verified` stays false until the exact value is in the source. The damage-formula section cites the pages from the user's 2026-10-22 research note for the crit and Vulnerable structure only. No other numbers were taken from those pages.

One exception is recorded on purpose. `data/meta-builds.json` has a single user-supplied Whirlwind name list and a d4builds link. That row stores names and the link only. It has no numeric guide data, `verified` is false, and it is not an open-license dataset. Do not add more rows from guide sites.

Search tests use invented numbers only in `test/fixtures/`, and those files are marked `SYNTHETIC`.

## Catalog

`data/schema/catalog.schema.json` is the class catalog. `data/barbarian.json` is the migrated v0 data plus the batch-2 video transcription. `data/schema/build.schema.json` is a build: skills, 13 gear slots (affixes plus one aspect or one unique), and stubs for boards, glyphs, and talismans. A slot may also carry an item name when the catalog has no sourced stats for that item. `data/meta-builds.json` is the novelty comparison file. Its one row is the Whirlwind name list above, and `example` is false. `data/builds.json` holds saved builds. The user's current build is in that file. Its character sheet is verified from a screenshot. Max Life on that sheet is 473, and life numbers inside skill tooltips are ignored.

A skill rank is the shown total: base ranks, capped at 15, plus bonus ranks from gear, talismans, and paragon. Whirlwind's shown total is 41. Its base and bonus split is unknown. Skills and upgrades have `allocated`. Only allocated nodes are active. The confirmed bar is Whirlwind, War Cry, Challenging Shout, Rallying Cry, Wrath of the Berserker, and Iron Skin, with the upgrades named in the catalog. `allocationVerified` on a skill, and `verified` on an upgrade, confirm that allocation only. Skill and effect `verified` flags still refer to the numbers. Flay and every other skill and upgrade outside that bar are `allocated: false` and are left out of needs-input. The current skill tree has no separate passive list. Those six skills and their upgrades are the complete skill setup.

Codex aspect values use `min + (max - min) * (level - 1) / (maxLevel - 1)`. The first endpoint is the value at rank 1 and the second is the value at the cap, in the order shown. A range printed high to low is stored that way and decreases as the rank rises. That curve matches Accelerating Aspect at 21/21, Anger Management at 9/11, Wildbolt at 21/21 on `[3.5, 1.5]`, and Aspect of Metamorphosis at 12/13 on `[10, 4]`. Aspect of Coagulation's earlier transcribed points do not match it, so those points stay as transcribed and that formula application is unverified. `upgrade.verified` means the formula reproduces the shown point. All 102 Codex aspects are available to this Barbarian, with source `video-frames` and `userConfirmed: true`. Their numbers stay unverified. Gear slots, conditions, and stacking rules are null because the frame table does not contain them. A bucket is null when that roll has no `[+]` or `[x]` tag. Wildbolt's 15%[x] has no bracket range. Numbers that the frame did not show are left out.

Each effect has a stat, a kind, a damage bucket, a point value or a `[min, max]` roll range, conditions, a stacking rule, a source, and a verified flag. A bucket stays null when the line has no `[+]` or `[x]` tag. The kind is taken from the line text, first match:

- Weapon DPS and damage per hit are `base-weapon`. The damage formula multiplies weapon damage by the skill coefficient. It does not average a per-hit range, so the shown range is kept and no midpoint is stored.
- Armor, Damage Reduction, damage taken, resistances, and Maximum Life are `defense`.
- Movement Speed and Unhindered are `mobility`.
- A gem effect, and a line about Teleport, are `utility`. Teleport stays utility when the number on that line is a resource cost.
- Other resource costs and gains, including Fury and Primary Resource, are `resource`.
- A remaining line that deals or increases damage is `damage`.

A bucket is required only for a `damage` line that has no tag, and for a line that has no kind yet. Defense, resource, mobility, utility, and base-weapon lines do not need one. The damage score skips those kinds even when a line carries a bucket tag.

Vulnerable and Overpower uptime can be stored as estimates. `uptime.estimate` marks those fractions, and the note records the duration divided by the cooldown. An estimate is used by the scorer and is not engine-blocking. Berserk uptime stays unset when the screenshot does not support a fraction. War Cry, Rallying Cry, and Iron Skin have `coeffPct` 0 because those tooltips deal no damage. Wrath of the Berserker has no direct hit. Its fire aura is 560% and its burn is 56% over 5s. The burn is damage over time. Both are applied for 15.8/54.32 of the time, and the 56% is not divided by 5 seconds. Overpower is 26%[+] per stack. The working stack count is 4 during Wrath, and those stacks linger 4s after Wrath ends, so `overpowerPct` is 104 and the Overpower uptime is (15.8 + 4) / 54.32. That uptime stays an estimate. The Earthstriker per-stack line is the same bonus and is not added a second time. Armor and resistance ratings stay ratings. Mitigation percents are stored only when a screenshot shows them. With Stone of Jordan equipped, Armor 33,715 reduces 78.2% of direct and damage-over-time damage, and every resistance of 21,218 reduces 85.9%. The mitigation cap on that screen is 90%. A sheet with Stone of Jordan removed is a comparison snapshot on the build. It is not the current build, and only the Cold line on that snapshot has a mitigation percent. Critical Strike Chance and the sheet Critical Strike Damage percent are the crit inputs. Additive gear crit lines stay inside that sheet percent. Gear `[x]` Critical Strike Damage Multiplier lines sum into one crit factor. Aspect, unique, and skill `[x]` crit lines multiply separately. `critAudit.reading` switches the low-confidence sheet and active-weapon readings. Channeling and Unstoppable can be stored as estimated uptimes. Fury above 50 is 1, confirmed by the user, and is no longer an estimate. Max Fury 826, Fury regeneration 85, and +52.5% Fury generation are town values and may be higher in combat. Fortified is 1. The user confirmed the character is almost always Fortified. Aspect of Channeling's equipped 138%[x] is the line that uptime applies. The codex 69%[x] effect stays on the aspect and is not applied with the imprint. Channeling applies when the scored skill is channeled. An untagged `increased damage` line is additive only when official Blizzard text already in this repo says so. The patch notes stored here do not state that rule, so those lines stay unbucketed. All of the other effect fields are null when the notes do not say them. A point value may sit outside its base range. Masterworking goes up to 25, and one affix gets a further boost at the cap. Horadric Cube transfiguration can raise a roll too. `masterworkLevel` and `transfigured` are optional fields on a gear slot. Socketed gems are their own collection, with an upgrade level and one effect per slot type. `src/schema.js` checks the subset of JSON Schema used by those files and does not reject a value that falls outside `range`.

Constraints live in the catalog: skill-bar size (null until a source states it), one aspect per slot, and uniques fixed to a sourced slot list. A unique with `slots: null` cannot be equipped. Playability minimums in the file are null, so they reject nothing until you set them on the optimizer page or in `checkBuild`.

## Damage formula

The optimizer uses the season 2 crit and Vulnerable formula restated for season 15 in the user's 2026-10-22 research note. Sources are [A], [B], and [C] in the table above.

Vulnerable targets take a base ×1.2 (high confidence). Sheet Vulnerable Damage stays in the additive bucket and applies only while the target is Vulnerable. Headline: the sheet percent does not include that base 20%. The alternate subtracts 20. `[x]` Vulnerable Damage Multiplier lines sum, then the factor is `1.2 × (1 + sum)` (medium confidence). Melted Heart's x35% is in that sum and is not also in the general `[x]` product. The alternate multiplies each `[x]` line on its own.

Headline crit factor is `(A + sheet crit damage) / A × (1 + sum of gear [x] crit) × each aspect, unique, or skill [x] crit`. A is that state's additive factor. At 100% crit chance the factor is used directly. The sheet percent already includes the base 50%, so the extra ×1.5 is left off (medium, [E]). Same-type gear [x] crit lines add, then multiply once: two x20% lines are ×1.4 (medium-high, [A]). An [x] crit line on an aspect, unique, or skill multiplies on its own (medium-high, [A]). Insight x310%, Deathshead x88%, and Ramaladni x44% are gear lines and sum to ×5.42. Two readings stay low confidence and are switched with `critAudit.reading`: `sheet-includes-gear-x` leaves the gear [x] lines out of the factor, and `active-weapon` keeps only the swinging weapon (Whirlwind's tooltip names Deathshead, planner slot `weapon-2`). `sheet-includes-gear-x-active-weapon` sets both. The flagged range is the min and max power across those readings. Headline is the most plausible. A damage-over-time component leaves the crit factor and every crit-damage [x] line off. A skill with a direct hit and a damage-over-time part scores those parts separately, and only the direct part crits. A component whose type is unset is flagged, and crit stays off that part.

Each engine-read sheet stat stores a `town` number and an optional `combat` number. `sheetContext` is `town` or `combat` and selects which reading the engine uses. A bare number is a town reading. When the context is `combat` and that side is empty, the engine uses the town number and lists the fallback on the result. `currentFury` feeds Ramaladni's per-point rate. When it is empty, the engine uses the resolved Max Fury and marks that default as an estimate. The saved build stays on `town` until combat numbers are entered. `docs/combat-sheet-template.md` lists the sheet fields to read from an in-combat screenshot.

## What the optimizer does

1. Enumerate skill cores whose tags match the filter, up to the skill-bar size or the core-size limit.
2. Rank aspects and uniques by `marginal()` from `src/engine.js` (marginal percent of a bucket times the item's point value), place them greedily, then run a beam search. The kept loadout is the higher of the two. Items with a null point value, an unknown condition, or a unique slot of null are not placed. Equipped paragon nodes and glyphs passed in with the build are scored. A glyph with a null level is not scored. A provisional glyph level is scored only when that glyph stores a level-scaling formula. Screenshot glyph tooltips for Marshal, Wrath, Imbiber, and Twister are scored at the printed totals. A per-5 rate is multiplied by attributes purchased in range only when the board stores that count. These boards do not, so those rates stay unscaled and are flagged. A damage line with null conditions is not scored. Decimator's Vulnerable and Overpower lines join those uptime states. Might's 8%[x] with a Two-Handed weapon is on when the arsenal has a two-handed weapon. Weapons Master's 75%[x] and Might's 26%[x] vs Healthy and Injured share one uptime. When `additivePct` is set, that sheet All Damage percent is the unconditional additive base. Screenshot conditional rows (element, Vulnerable, Elites, Healthy or Injured, while Healthy, while Fortified) join the same sum with their uptimes. Other additive-bucket lines are not added on top. Equipped weapon affixes are copied onto every candidate, including the [x] Critical Strike Damage Multiplier lines the scorer reads. The search varies aspect and unique placement. Which paragon nodes, glyphs, boards, and talismans to equip is not searched. Warbringer's 45%[x] applies while Fortified.
3. Reject a build that breaks slot constraints or misses a minimum you set. Fury sustain is generation per second minus spend per second for the rotation. Berserk, Vulnerable, and Overpower uptime are fractions you enter (0–1). EHP is `life / ((1 - armor mitigation) × (1 - resist mitigation) × Π(1 - DR))` using mitigation percents you read off the character sheet. Armor and resist ratings are not converted, because that curve is not in the sourced data.
4. Novelty is the distance to the nearest non-example row in `data/meta-builds.json`: the average of Jaccard distance (skills, aspects, uniques, key items) and normalized bucket-profile distance. The shipped row has a null bucket profile, so only the name-list distance is used. With no real meta rows, novelty is 1. Rank is power × novelty, after a power floor. Fully verified builds are the default. The unverified-data toggle shows the rest, each with its unverified share. The page lists up to 20 and prints the engine's why breakdown.

Manual weapon, main stat, and crit inputs count as unverified until you check that you verified them in game. Checking the box does not create a source.

## Editing data

Use the data-entry page, or edit `data/barbarian.json` directly. Export from the page and replace the file. Set `verified: true` only after the exact value is in an official source, and keep the source URL on the entity and the effect.

The v0 calculator uses the same engine: base × additive × multiplicative × main stat × crit × Vulnerable × overpower. Vulnerable is ×1.2 times the summed [x] Vulnerable lines. Crit is (A + sheet crit damage) / A times the gear [x] sum times each separate [x] crit line. DoT skips crit. Bucket names ending in `?` are not used until you confirm them. Builds and votes stay in localStorage.
